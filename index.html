USDC is the gas token on Arc
Arc is a Layer 2 blockchain where USDC is the native gas token. You pay fees in USDC — no ETH needed. The same USDC balance covers gas and token swaps.
50% auto-lock mechanism
Every swap locks 50% of your $SAVIOR for 5–10 days (random). The rest hits your wallet instantly. This reduces sell pressure and rewards patient holders.
Uniswap V4 Hook enforcement
All swaps are routed through SaviorHook on Uniswap V4. Direct swaps that bypass the staking contract are blocked at the hook level. 2% of each swap goes to treasury.
Emergency unlock (admin)
Only the admin can trigger emergencyUnlockAll(). This sets globalUnlock=true permanently — all locked positions become instantly claimable. Cannot be reversed.
