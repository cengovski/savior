Fresh start: new SAVIOR token, pool and staking

Ordered steps 1 to 5 (matches staking-v2/script/fresh/DeployFresh.s.sol). Every write is simulated with eth_call first and then signed by your wallet. Only the deployer 0x7185...20e7 can run steps. A step unlocks only after the previous one verifies on-chain.

Not connected
SAVIOR transfers are blocked in this panel until STEP 4 finishes: the deployer balance must equal the approved ladder amount.
