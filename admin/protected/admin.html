Ordered steps 1 to 6 (same order as staking-v2/script/fresh/DeployFresh.s.sol). Every write is simulated with eth_call first and then signed by your wallet. Only the deployer 0x7185...20e7 can run steps. A step unlocks only after the previous one verifies on-chain. ethers is served from this origin with SRI.
