How it works

SAVIOR is a token on Arc Mainnet, the stablecoin-native chain where USDC is the gas token. It trades in a Uniswap V4 pool (SAVIOR / USDC) protected by a custom hook. Swaps go through the SaviorStaking contract, which applies the rules below.

  1. You pay USDC. The pool swaps it for SAVIOR.
  2. 50% of the SAVIOR goes to your wallet right away.
  3. The other 50% is locked in the staking contract for a random 5 to 10 days.
  4. When a lock expires, you claim it from the Locks section of the app.

Selling works the other way around: you send SAVIOR and receive USDC. Sells are not locked. Selling in the same block as a buy is not allowed.

Fees

Every swap pays two fees. The app's estimate already includes both.

Pool fee (to liquidity providers)1%
Hook fee (to the treasury)0.3% of the output
Network gaspaid in USDC, usually cents

Slippage. The default tolerance is 0.5%. You can pick 0.1%, 0.5%, 1% or a custom value. If the price moves more than that before your swap lands, the swap reverts and you only pay gas. Above 1% the app shows a warning.

Small pool. Liquidity is still small, so large trades move the price a lot. If an amount is bigger than the pool can fill, the app tells you before you sign.

Locks and claims

Each buy creates one lock that holds half of the SAVIOR you bought. Each lock has its own unlock time between 5 and 10 days after the buy.

  • See your locks: connect your wallet and open My Locked Positions. You will see the amount, a countdown and the status of each lock. Times are shown in your local time zone.
  • Claim: when a lock shows Ready, press Claim and confirm in your wallet. The SAVIOR goes straight to your wallet.
  • Nothing expires: an unlocked position stays claimable. You do not lose it if you wait.
  • Leaderboard: the public Locks Leaderboard shows every wallet's locked total, read directly from the staking contract. Locks unlocking within 24 hours are highlighted.

Bridge USDC to Arc

The Bridge section moves native USDC from another chain to Arc with Circle CCTP (Arc is CCTP domain 26). It uses Circle Bridge Kit in your browser. Your wallet signs every step, and no one else holds your funds.

  1. Approve: allow USDC to be burned on the source chain.
  2. Burn on source: the USDC is burned on the source chain.
  3. Attestation: Circle signs a message confirming the burn.
  4. Mint on Arc: the same amount of native USDC is minted to your address on Arc.

You need USDC and gas on the source chain, plus a little USDC on Arc to pay for the mint. Fast transfers finish in minutes for a small fee. Standard transfers have no fast fee but wait for source-chain finality. If a transfer stops partway, use Retry last transfer.

Supported source chains: Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Avalanche, Polygon PoS, Linea and Unichain.

Risks

SAVIOR is an experimental meme token. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

  • You can lose everything. The price can drop to zero. Only use money you can afford to lose.
  • Smart contract risk. The token, hook, staking and bridge contracts can have bugs. They have not been formally audited.
  • Locked funds. Half of every buy is locked for 5 to 10 days. You cannot sell locked SAVIOR until you claim it, even if the price falls.
  • Low liquidity. The pool is small, so prices move sharply and large sells may not be possible.
  • Bridge risk. Bridging relies on Circle CCTP and on the source chain. Delays or outages can hold up a transfer.
  • Network risk. Arc is a new network. Public RPC endpoints can be slow or rate-limited, so data in the app may lag.
  • Your keys, your responsibility. Never share your seed phrase. Always check what you sign.
